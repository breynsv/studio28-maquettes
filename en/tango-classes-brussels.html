Tango Barre: the tango and the barre

The class mixes the vocabulary of tango with barre work: the footing, the axis, the slowness. It is as much a posture class as a dance class, and that is what makes it workable without a partner.

An hour, Wednesday from 18:00 to 19:00, just before the room tips over into the evening classes.

Among women

The class is taught among women. That is not an administrative restriction but the frame of the class, and it is what those who sign up come for.

Booking goes through IMPULS

Like the children's classes, Tango Barre is taught by the IMPULS school. Booking and payment go through them, and the LDE card does not apply. The link to their site is in the timetable below.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to bring a partner?
No. The work is done at the barre and among the participants.
Do I need to have danced tango before?
No, the class starts from posture and footing.
Can I pay with my credits?
No: the class is taught by IMPULS and paid to them.
When is it?
Wednesday from 18:00 to 19:00.
