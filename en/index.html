11 — Frequently asked questions

The questions
we get asked

Still got one? Message us on WhatsApp, we answer the same day.

Can I come on my own? +

Yes. We rotate partners in every dance class, so you don't need to bring anyone.

I have never danced / never done yoga. Is that a problem? +

No. Every discipline has its beginner class, and you can start at any point in the year — not just in September.

Do I need a subscription? +

No. You buy credits and spend them where you like, when you like.

Are you open during the school holidays? +

Yes, on a lighter timetable. It is published here two weeks in advance.

What should I bring? +

A bottle of water and comfortable clothes. Mats are provided for yoga and pilates; for dance, bring clean shoes.

Current prices and offers +

All our prices are shown on the Prices page — they are always up to date.

The teachers at Studio 28

L

Laura

Pilates · Salsa

For her, pilates is as much introspection as exercise: posture, conscious breathing, alignment, mobility. You gain core strength and ease aches.

M

Marvin

Yoga

Fifteen years of practice, ten of teaching. Trained in India, he has taught in Germany, France, Singapore, Thailand and Nigeria. Well-structured sequences, alignment, breathing.

Selasi

Hip-hop

Selasi Dogbatse: ballet at 4, hip-hop at 12, Los Angeles at 20. She has danced for Rihanna, Beyoncé, Missy Elliott, Cardi B and Drake. Her show A Piece of Me played at Bozar.

Elena

Heels

Elena Gambardella, a pioneer of heels in Belgium and the creator of the Heels & Confidence method. X Factor, Eurovision, The Voice, Nike; Booba, Angèle, Oscar and the Wolf.
